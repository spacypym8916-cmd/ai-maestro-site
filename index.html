AI Maestro

A private desktop app an independent creator uses to publish music playlists on their own YouTube channels.

AI Maestro is not sold or distributed, and only its developer uses it. It automates the repetitive parts of running the developer's own music channels:

  • uploading finished playlist videos with their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, localized metadata and captions;
  • adding videos to the channel's playlists;
  • running 24/7 live playlist streams, and rotating each stream about every 11 hours so every session is kept as a replay;
  • adding lyric captions to those replays and posting a first comment and an occasional chat message.

Every video and broadcast is marked as altered or synthetic content.
