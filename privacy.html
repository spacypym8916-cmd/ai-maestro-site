Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

AI Maestro is a private desktop application used only by its developer to manage the developer's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

AI Maestro uses YouTube API Services. By using AI Maestro you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

  • The developer's own channels (with OAuth consent): channel information, videos, playlists, captions, live broadcasts and streams, and the comments and live chat messages the app posts.
  • Public data from other channels: titles and view counts of public videos on reference channels or in search results, used only as style references when writing new video titles.

The app does not access any other person's account, private data or personal information.

How the data is used

Only to upload and manage videos, captions, playlists and live streams on the developer's own channels, and to write titles and descriptions for them.

Sharing

To write titles and descriptions, the app sends a text request to an AI text-generation service chosen by the developer (for example OpenAI or Google Gemini). That request can include the public video titles described above. Nothing else from YouTube is sent to these services. No data is sold, and no data is shared with advertisers.

Storage and retention

  • All data stays on the developer's own computers. There is no server.
  • OAuth tokens are encrypted with the operating system's secure storage.
  • Public data from other channels (titles and view counts), including copies in the app's request logs, is deleted within 30 days.

Revoking access and deletion

Disconnecting a channel in the app deletes its stored token. Access can also be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. After that the app can no longer access the channel.
